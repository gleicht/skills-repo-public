# KDP category browse-path catalog — US

A catalog of selectable Amazon US category **browse paths**, imported from the
user's Publisher Rocket category export. **Paths only — no competition data.** For
sales-annotated categories (sales-to-#1/#10, KU%), see
[`kdp-categories-us.md`](kdp-categories-us.md), which is the source of truth for any
genre it covers (currently Science Fiction & Fantasy — omitted here to avoid
duplication).

- **Source:** Publisher Rocket category export (`amz_categories.xlsx`), Amazon.com (US).
- **Imported:** 2026-06-13 (Mystery branch expanded in a later revision same day).
- `Books ›` paths are the **print** tree; `Kindle Books ›` paths are the **Kindle**
  tree. Pick from the one matching the format you are categorizing.
- Still confirm any node in the live KDP picker before publishing; Amazon renames and
  reshuffles categories.
