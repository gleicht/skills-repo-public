# KDP categories — US (real data, Publisher Rocket)

Authoritative, **sales-annotated** Amazon US category data, pasted from a Publisher
Rocket Category Search. Prefer this file over the curated tree in
[`category-map.md`](category-map.md) for any genre it covers — these are real node
names and competitiveness numbers, not a training-time guess. Still confirm a node is
present in the live KDP picker for your book's **format** before publishing.

- **Source:** Publisher Rocket Category Search, Amazon.com (US).
- **Imported:** 2026-06-13.
- **Genres covered so far:** Science Fiction & Fantasy. *To add another, paste a Rocket
  Category Search export for it and it gets a section here.*

## How to read the columns

- **→#1 / →#10** — approximate copies/day the current #1 (or #10) book in that
  category sells. **Lower means easier to rank.** For a launch chasing a bestseller
  badge, target categories with a low →#1 (or at least a low →#10) that genuinely fit
  the book.
- **Pub%** — share of the category's top books from traditional/large publishers. Lower
  is more indie-friendly (easier for a self-published book to break into the top).
- **KU%** (Kindle rows only) — share of top books enrolled in Kindle Unlimited. High KU%
  signals a KU/read-through readership (common in romance and genre fantasy).
- **dup** — Rocket flags this node as a near-duplicate of another; picking both wastes
  one of your category slots.
- Every row below was marked **selectable** (pickable directly in KDP).

## Print vs. Kindle naming

The print ("Books ›") and Kindle ("Kindle Books ›") trees are **not identical** — pick
from the tree that matches the format you're categorizing. Watch the divergences:
`Dark` (print) vs `Dark Fantasy` (Kindle); `Alternate History` vs `Alternative
History`; `Superheroes` vs `Superhero`; `Anthologies` vs `Anthologies & Short
Stories`; `Exploration`/`Space Opera` (print SF) vs `Space Exploration`/`Space Opera`
(Kindle SF). Kindle also adds nodes print lacks (`Christian Fantasy`, `Classics`,
`Fairy Tales`, `Indigenous`, `Metaphysical & Visionary`).
